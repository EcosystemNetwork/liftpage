# Brand — LiftPage

Status: live

Tagline: Attention into traction.

LiftPage is an AI-first social desk for technology startups, founders, hackathons, and innovation communities. The interface is a call sheet, not a marketing template.

## Direction

Industrial / utilitarian. Compact. Flat panels, 1px lines, no card shadows.

## Color

| Token | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ink | `#12110E` | page |
| panel | `#1A1814` | the campaign card and selected row |
| paper | `#F3EDE3` | text |
| mute | `#C8BFAF` | secondary text |
| line | `#343027` | hairlines |
| tape | `#E4FF3A` | the name, the price, the cue |
| heat | `#FF5C38` | errors |

No pure black. Tape is the only accent.

## Type

- Schibsted Grotesk for the company and the captions
- IBM Plex Mono for days, prices, and statuses

## Voice

Short lines. Name the client, the day, and the offer. No fake metrics. No "excited to announce".

## Motion

The desk rises once, 360ms. Reduced motion kills it.
